# Landing page (/)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: owners of small appointment shops, often on a phone between clients. Action: try the live demo (one click per role). Proof: the working demo, the database-level no-double-booking guarantee, the open repo. No invented customers, numbers or pricing.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A barbershop fade-night flyer that sells one fact: a slot sells once. It refuses the booking-SaaS default (soft gradient, calendar screenshot, feature-card grid) and the black-and-gold "luxury barber" opposite.

OWN-WORLD: Scarlet #e3140f fields, black #0b0b0b cut-paper shards, newsprint #f4f1ea, halftone grey. Barlow Condensed 800 italic caps at poster scale, Barlow for reading. Square corners, sheared tags (-9deg), torn edges only on the hero shards, halftone over duotone photos, no soft shadows, no glass.

STORY: The visitor sees times being taken and a second claim smashed away, reads "Never double-booked", understands it is real booking software for their shop, scrolls through what clients, staff and owners get, and walks into the demo as any role. No code or tech-stack sections (owner decision).

FIRST VIEWPORT: Scarlet field edge to edge. Black nav strip on top (wordmark, Features, Live demo, GitHub, Log in, Try the live demo); the nav carries the action, so the hero has no duplicate buttons. Left 55%: H1 "Fully booked. Never double-booked." in condensed italic caps, about 8.5vw, two lines sheared; one line of copy. Right 45%: the slot wall, twelve torn black time shards in 3D settling into a tilted grid; 10:00 flips white "BOOKED"; a rival shard for 10:00 is smashed flat with a TAKEN stamp; 10:30 lights as next free. Static DOM board of the same state on mobile, reduced motion, low power, no WebGL.

FORM: Rebel stylish-menu collage fused with a barbershop flyer; challenger from the dealt hand, chosen by the owner. seed:4c3c9146582c2c0e

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
